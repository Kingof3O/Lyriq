# Screen blueprints

Canonical visual references are in `01_Screens/`.

## 01 Reader Dark
Use for the default app experience. Key characteristics:
- no sidebar
- track identity in top center
- stage status chips top-left
- five-ish lyric lines with a single strong active line
- active rail is the signature motif
- floating bottom dock

## 02 Study Lens
Reader remains visible and meaningful. The right inspector is 358 px and compresses stage width. Word selection uses underline, not a big filled highlight.

## 03 Lyrics Search
Dedicated full-window workspace. Candidate list left; explanation inspector right. Selection is indicated by a rail and faint wash.

## 04 Command Palette
Opaque carbon panel, not glass. Underlying Reader is dimmed. Selected command has rail, not a bright rounded rectangle.

## 05 Settings
Text-only category rail. Main settings are hairline-separated rows. Appearance preview is a live specimen, not a thumbnail card grid.

## 06 Mini Island
Independent composition. Track metadata is secondary, active lyric is primary. Progress is fused to bottom edge.

## 07 Ghost Overlay
No chrome. Two lyric tiers only. A temporary status hint confirms click-through state.

## 08 Reader Light
Same information architecture as dark Reader. Do not redesign layout just because theme changes.
